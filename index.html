Shallow-water simulation running live on your GPU. The boat is unstable: keep your torso up with A/D — small taps, all the way down. Paddle with the arrow keys. Watch your stamina: tired paddlers are slow and weak. The first 30 m are a calm pool. Spinning paddles (xp) and coins float above the water — grab them as you pass, they fade away soon after you get close. Loot only banks if you reach the take-out.
