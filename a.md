
Let's also add a built bridge. It is possible to configure its material and color. There is a road extending from both ends of the bridge as far as the eye can see. You might need to adjust terrain geometry for the road too, but at least do it with texturing.

The built bridge can have thin pylons that come in pairs (one on each side of the bridge) or then whole bridge width single pylons. It will not have other props on top of it. Otherwise the logic should work like with the natural bridge.
